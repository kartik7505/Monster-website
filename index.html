FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about the Ultra formulation, taste profile, and energy matrix.

Monster Ultra (commonly known as “White Monster”) features an ultra-crisp, light, sparkling citrus profile with subtle notes of grapefruit and lime. It is distinctly less syrupy and lighter than original Monster Energy, providing immediate refreshment with zero sticky sugar residue.

Monster Ultra contains exactly 0 grams of sugar and only 10 calories per 350ml serving. It delivers 150mg of pure caffeine, supplemented with our proprietary blend of Taurine, Panax Ginseng, L-Carnitine, and essential B-Vitamins for sustained focus without insulin spikes.

Monster Ultra is best consumed ice-cold approximately 20 to 30 minutes before high-intensity workouts, competitive gaming sessions, long driving shifts, or whenever sharp cognitive alertness is required. We recommend not exceeding 400mg of caffeine daily from all sources.
